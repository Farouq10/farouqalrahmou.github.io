Scope

Prepared for Pearson Edexcel GCSE History: Medicine in Britain, c1250–present and the British sector of the Western Front, 1914–18 (option 11), and Superpower relations and the Cold War, 1941–91 (option P4). Edexcel is inferred from the original published deck and should be checked against your course.

The deck covers major dates and their significance, including wider context. There is no official exhaustive list of “Grade 9 dates”. Dates alone do not establish a Grade 9 answer: practise explanations, comparisons, supported judgements and source evaluation using your course materials.

How cards are delivered

The original successive-relearning algorithm is preserved. New sessions shuffle the deck. Very easy and Easy retire a never-missed card. After a mistake, a card needs two later correct answers; another mistake resets that streak. All unseen cards are attempted before mistakes repeat. Once that pass ends, Not quite, No idea and Impossible reinsert the card after 4, 2 or 1 intervening cards respectively, or at the end of a shorter queue. The two correct ratings reinsert a previously missed card after 12 or 7 cards until corrected twice.

This is session practice, not FSRS or a calendar-based spaced-repetition scheduler. Completed cards return when you choose Study again.

Progress

Every rating saves in localStorage and IndexedDB before the displayed card advances. Browser storage is specific to this hostname and browser; clearing site data can remove both copies. Download a progress backup for an independent copy or to move devices. Progress on the old GitHub hostname cannot automatically be read by the new Cloudflare hostname.

Firebase sync is optional and currently unconfigured. It requires a Firebase Web app configuration, Google sign-in, authorised domains and Firestore rules restricting writes to the signed-in owner's document. Do not publish private service account credentials.
