Study method and scope

Back to dates

159 date-only cards: 75 Medicine including Western Front, and 84 Cold War. Edexcel options 11 and P4. Dates use day/month precision for distinct events and year/range precision for discoveries and developments where that is appropriate. This is a substantial revision set, not an official exhaustive list.

FSRS estimates difficulty, stability and retrievability. Target recall is 90%, using the upstream default parameters. Parameters are not trained on your personal data. Four ratings preserve FSRS semantics: Again means wrong; Hard means correct with difficulty; Good means correct; Easy means effortless. A wrong answer should be Again, not Hard.

The five stacks are a display of memory stability, not a five-box scheduling algorithm. Stack 1 holds new/learning/relearning cards; the others group review stability below 3, 7, 21 days, and at least 21 days. Due cards are ordered by predicted recall. Study ahead offers future-due cards without falsifying the review time. Early reviews may be less efficient than spaced reviews. The stack thresholds and ordering are interface choices, not claims of experimentally optimal settings.

The bundled MIT-licensed ts-fsrs source is pinned to commit c8ca282edc3fe1cdfa1c24912437938b63a25cb3. Benchmarks evaluate recall prediction, not a guarantee of higher exam grades or universally optimal learning.

Progress saves to localStorage and IndexedDB before moving on. Clearing site data can delete both: use the optional backup. Accounts and cloud sync are absent. Earlier successive-relearning snapshots are kept untouched under their original storage keys; their completion counters do not contain the dated review history needed to reconstruct FSRS memory safely.
