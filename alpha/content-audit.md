# Alpha card audit

Reviewed all 408 existing cards: 208 English and 200 History. There are 150 unique preserved-ID corrections.

- All 120 poetry prompts now identify their poem, fixing ambiguous mixed-review prompts.
- 78 English analytical backs were improved around precise methods, argument, comparison and justified uncertainty. Many overlap the 120 prompt changes.
- Seven History cards were clarified or corrected: Himmler’s 1936 police appointment versus earlier Gestapo control; the Black Death’s arrival versus outbreak range; compulsory sanitation duties; Mary’s existing English claim; reconnaissance versus colony; and two Gregorian/Old Style Armada dates.
- Four Inspector Calls context prompts distinguish the 1912 setting from post-war reception without implying a British première in 1945. The Soviet première was in 1945; the British première followed in 1946.
- No existing card IDs were removed. No new poem quotations were added. Existing short quotations in prompts remain unchanged apart from the added poem identifier.

This was a complete reading and targeted editorial/factual audit, not independent primary-source verification of every History date or official mark-scheme validation. Most History cards were retained. Source URLs on substantive historical corrections point to the supporting museum, historical-site or parliamentary evidence. The literature changes are original analysis of the supplied material.

Use only the front, back, topic and date fields to patch card content. The reason and source fields are audit metadata. Preserving IDs retains scheduling history, although an updated answer may deserve an early review.
